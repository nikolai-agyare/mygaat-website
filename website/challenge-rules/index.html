Monthly challenge rules

Last updated 5 November 2026

The monthly challenge has not started yet. These rules show how it will work. We will confirm the final rules and the first challenge date in the app before anyone can enter.

Free to enter

The challenge is free. No purchase or subscription is needed to enter or to win, and subscribing does not improve your chances. Everyone gets the same one attempt per subject.

Who can enter

  • myGAAT account holders who live in the United Kingdom.
  • You must unlock the challenge first by revising on 10 days in a row (UK time). A day counts when you finish a practice battle or a flashcard deck while signed in. Free players can do this with their free daily battles. Once unlocked, the challenge stays unlocked, even if your streak ends later.
  • Students in Year 10 to Year 13, aged 13 to 19.
  • If you are under 18, a parent or carer must agree, using the code we email them, before you can enter.
  • People who run myGAAT, and their families, cannot enter.

Prizes

Each month there are 15 prizes of £100: one for each of these groups.

Year group Subjects
Year 10 Maths, Biology, Chemistry, Physics and English Literature
Year 11
A level (Year 12 and 13)

You can enter each subject you take. There is no cash alternative or swap for a different prize.

How it works

  1. The challenge runs on the last weekend of each month, UK time.
  2. You get one official 2-minute attempt for each of your subjects.
  3. Your score is the number of correct answers minus the number of wrong answers (lowest score 0). Skipping a question does not count against you.
  4. The highest score in each group wins.

If there is a tie

  1. The student with the higher accuracy (correct answers out of answers given) wins.
  2. If still tied, the tied students play a tie-break round.

Winners are never picked at random.

Winning

  • We tell winners in the app and by email within 7 days of the challenge ending.
  • If you are under 18, we also contact your parent or carer to agree and to arrange payment.
  • You need to reply within 28 days. If you do not, the prize goes to the next highest score.
  • We may show winners' usernames in the app. We never show real names.

Fair play

We check results before giving out prizes. If we find cheating, such as getting help from someone else, using more than one account, or using tools to answer questions, we will remove that entry and may close the account. Our decision on results is final, but you can always ask us to explain it.

Apple and Google

Apple and Google are not sponsors of the monthly challenge and are not involved in it in any way.

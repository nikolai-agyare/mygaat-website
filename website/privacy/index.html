Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information myGAAT collects, why, and what you can do about it. We have tried to keep it short and clear. If anything is unclear, email support@mygaat.co.uk.

The short version. We collect as little as we can. Other students only ever see your username, your avatar and your scores. Free players see a few ads, which are never personalised and never based on what you do. We never sell your information and never ask for your real name, school or location.

Who we are

myGAAT is a revision app run by Nikolai Agyare in the United Kingdom. Nikolai is the "controller" of your information, which means he decides how it is used and is responsible for keeping it safe. If myGAAT moves to a company, we will update this policy and tell you in the app. You can contact us at support@mygaat.co.uk.

Who myGAAT is for

myGAAT is for students in Year 10 to Year 13 (aged 13 to 19). You cannot sign up if you are under 13. If you are under 18, we ask for a parent or carer's email when you sign up.

What we collect

Information Why we need it
Your email address and password, or your Apple or Google sign-in To create your account and let you sign in. Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed), so we cannot read them.
Your username To show your scores on leaderboards without showing who you are.
Whether you are under 18 To decide whether we need a parent or carer's email. We check your date of birth on your phone and only keep a yes or no, not the date.
A parent or carer's email (under 18s only) To tell them about your account and, in future, to ask for their agreement where the law needs it.
Your school year, subjects and exam boards To show you the right questions and put you in the right leaderboard.
Your scores, XP and when you played To run battles, leaderboards and the monthly challenge.
Which days you revised To count your revision streak and unlock the monthly challenge.
Your avatar look and outfit To show your avatar in the app and on leaderboards.
Whether you subscribe To unlock subscriber features. Apple or Google take the payment and we never see your card details.
Messages you send us To answer your questions and reports.

Some things are stored only on your phone, such as notifications, light or dark mode, and the list of questions you got wrong for "Review my mistakes".

We do not collect your real name, school, address, location, contacts, photos or microphone. We do not use tracking tools.

Ads

If you don't subscribe, myGAAT shows a few ads: one you can choose to watch for an extra battle, and sometimes one after a battle ends. Ads never appear during a battle. Subscribers never see ads.

  • Ads come from Google AdMob. We ask Google for non-personalised ads only, so ads are not based on your interests or what you do in other apps.
  • We ask for ads rated suitable for teenagers, and we block gambling, dating and similar ads.
  • Students under 18 are marked as under the age of consent, so Google treats their information with extra care.
  • To show and count ads, and to stop fraud, Google receives your device's IP address and basic device information, and may use your phone's advertising ID. We never give Google your email, username, age or scores.
  • You can reset or delete your phone's advertising ID in your phone's settings, or subscribe to remove ads.

What other people can see

Other students can see your username, your avatar and your best scores on the leaderboard for your year and subject. They cannot see your email, your age or anything else. You can hide yourself from leaderboards in the Me tab.

Our legal reasons for using your information

  • To provide the app you signed up for (contract): your account, battles, leaderboards and subscriptions.
  • Legitimate interests: showing non-personalised ads to free players, which pays for the free version of the app.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the app safe and fair, such as blocking rude usernames and stopping cheating.
  • Legal obligation: keeping records the law requires, such as prize payments.

Who we share it with

We only share information with companies that help us run myGAAT, and only what they need:

  • Supabase stores our database and handles sign-in and sign-in emails.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in if you choose it, app downloads and subscription payments.
  • RevenueCat keeps track of whether your subscription is active.
  • Resend sends the code email to a parent or carer when a student under 18 subscribes.
  • Google AdMob shows ads to free players, as explained in the Ads section above. Read how Google uses information from apps.
  • Our email provider handles messages sent to support@mygaat.co.uk.

Some of these companies may store information outside the UK. When they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK data protection law. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. When you ask us to delete it, we delete your account and everything linked to it within 30 days. If you win a prize, we keep a record of the prize for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop or limit how we use it

Email support@mygaat.co.uk to do any of these. A parent or carer can also ask on behalf of a child. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we use your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
